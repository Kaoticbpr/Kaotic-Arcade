The two .so files here are libretro emulator cores, downloaded
unmodified from the official libretro buildbot:

    https://buildbot.libretro.com/nightly/android/latest/arm64-v8a/

  fbneo_libretro_android.so     FinalBurn Neo   - arcade (CPS-1/2/3,
                                Neo Geo and many others)
                                https://github.com/libretro/FBNeo
                                Free for non-commercial use.

  flycast_libretro_android.so   Flycast         - Sega NAOMI and
                                Dreamcast
                                https://github.com/flyinghead/flycast
                                GPLv2.

They are included so the installer can set everything up without
you having to fetch them through RetroArch's Online Updater.
Nothing here has been modified.
